## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

The first release.

### Methods that drive a goal
- Mandala 64: Ohtani's 9×9 chart as a star map, with eight pillars and 64 actions. Actions can be habits or one-off steps, a star lights up when one has become part of you, and the chart can be shared as a poster.
- OKR: an objective, key results with start and target, check-ins with history, a 0–1 score, confidence and initiatives.
- 12-week year: weekly tactics, an execution score for every week against the 85 % line, a vision, and a review week that starts the next year.
- 4DX: the wildly important goal, lead measures on a weekly scoreboard, the last eight weeks and a weekly commitment.
- 1% a day: a daily target that compounds, a chart of target against what you did, streaks and projections.
- 30-day pact: a star for each day, daily notes, and persist, pause or pivot at the end.
- Reverse plan: a ladder of dated steps from the finish back to today.
- Milestones: an optional number, milestones and habits.

### Lenses for any goal
- SMART, WOOP with if-then plans and reminders, GROW, 5 whys and a pre-mortem.

### Choosing a goal
- Wheel of life with history, Odyssey plans, 5/25 and Dreamline. Any result can become a goal.
- "Which method fits me?": five questions suggest two methods and a lens.
- Every method explains what it is, its steps and where it comes from, where you choose it and on the goal it drives.

### Every day
- Today's course: steps from all goals on one screen, with streaks, counters and kaizen numbers, and a backup course of if-then plans.
- Goals drawn as constellations that light up as they move; reached goals stay in the journal.
- A weekly review, a focus limit with a queue, a home-screen widget, and morning, evening, weekly and per-step reminders.
- English and Russian, night and dawn themes, a JSON backup. No account, no ads, no internet access.
